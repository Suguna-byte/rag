# Behavioural Answers and Interview Strategy

## STAR format
Situation, Task, Action, Result. Keep the situation short, spend most time on your actions, and end with a measurable or concrete result. Prepare five stories: a hard bug, a conflict or disagreement, a time you learned something fast, a project you owned end to end, and a mistake you made and fixed.

## Story bank from your experience
- Hard bug: duplicate submissions in CRM visit tracking. Root cause was double clicks and retries creating repeat records; fix was a frontend in-flight guard plus a backend uniqueness or idempotency check.
- Learning fast: moving into RAG and LLM applications for the NABARD report platform.
- End-to-end ownership: Verve Studio client projects, from requirements to deployment.
- Working with business rules: HR leave and payroll penalty logic, where you had to clarify ambiguous policies with stakeholders.

## Why AI engineering
Connect your story: you built enterprise apps, then RAG and ML projects, and you want to build AI systems that work reliably in production. Mention that full-stack skills matter in AI engineering because most AI value is delivered through APIs, data pipelines and user interfaces.

## How to answer when you do not know
Say what you do know, reason from first principles out loud, and say how you would find out. Never bluff; interviewers test the edges of your knowledge on purpose.

## Questions to ask the interviewer
- What does the AI stack look like today, and what is the biggest problem with it?
- How do you evaluate model and prompt changes before shipping?
- What would success look like for this role in the first 90 days?
- How much of the work is building new features vs improving quality of existing AI features?

## Final-day checklist
- Re-read your resume line by line and be able to explain every word.
- Be ready to draw your NABARD RAG architecture on a whiteboard.
- Practice explaining BM25, embeddings, hybrid search, reranking and evaluation in two minutes each.
- Prepare one demo: the local RAG system in this folder, and the trade-offs you would change in production.
